# Our reader finds the training examples that teach a model to give in

**From the lab** · published 2026-11-15 · `sycophancy` `reading inside the model` `training data` `work in progress`

> Among replies written to teach firmness, the internal signal flags the ones that teach the opposite: 12 of its top 30, none of its bottom 30.

We pointed the internal signal of sycophancy at our own training data. Among 1,500 replies written to teach a model to hold its ground, it put at the top the ones that actually give in: 12 of its top 30, none of its bottom 30. A way to clean training data before spending GPU time on it.

**Read the full article, with the method and its limits: [https://splabs.io/lab/reader-finds-bad-training-examples](https://splabs.io/lab/reader-finds-bad-training-examples)**
