# October's human factor: fake fines, PagoPA on certified mail, and AI tools on the list

**AI security and psychology** · published 2026-11-06 · `AI security` `human factor` `phishing` `CPF3`

> What a month of attacks that need a person to fall for them says about the levers that work.

In four October weeks Italy's national CERT analysed 755 malicious campaigns. The ones that stood out pulled the oldest levers in the book: a police fine to pay right away, a PagoPA notice sent from a real certified mailbox. And for the first time, AI tools appear on the list of vulnerabilities. A month read through the CPF3 indicators.
