# A model's bad habits are not independent

**From the lab** · published 2026-10-28 · `drift` `reading inside the model` `instrument panel` `work in progress`

> Read side by side, a model's drift habits turn out to be related in a pattern, not separate alarms.

We read several of a model's drift habits as internal signals and, for the first time, read them together on one panel. Each signal stays specific to its own habit, so a panel does not blur them; but two of them, grandiosity and a detached register, rise and fall together even on ordinary text, while caving sits on its own axis. The habits have a structure, not a flat list, and the relationships between them are information a single gauge hides.

**Read the full article, with the method and its limits: [https://splabs.io/lab/a-models-bad-habits-are-not-independent](https://splabs.io/lab/a-models-bad-habits-are-not-independent)**

## The numbers

### How related are the habits? (on ordinary text, no drift present)

| | Move together |
|---|---:|
| Grandiosity + detachment | 0.34 |
| Caving + grandiosity | 0.22 |
| Caving + detachment | 0.09 |

_0 means unrelated, higher means the two signals rise and fall together._
